Privacy & Data

Google AdSense Advertising Disclosure: We deploy third-party advertising modules handled by Google AdSense. Google uses operational web cookies to display relevant advertisements to visitors based on browsing history logs on this platform or external digital channels.

1. Tracking & Opt-Out Protocols

Google's integration of tracking features lets its network push relevant ad layout units directly into your user environment. You can explicitly choose to decline or turn off personalized interest tracking filters at any time by accessing your profile preferences panel inside Google Ads Settings.

2. System Log Files

Velopipe Hub logs routine diagnostic page metrics (browser versions, time stamps, content view durations) to compute scroll visibility values. This performance data is entirely separate from ad profiling engines.

3. Consent Framework

By interacting with our dynamic pipeline navigation system, you acknowledge and accept our automated resource configurations and third-party monetization parameters.

4. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your residence location, you may have specific legal rights regarding your Personal Data:

  • The right to access the data collected and published.
  • The right to request detailed information about the specific types of Data we've collected over the past 12 months, including data disclosed for business purposes.
  • The right to request to rectify or update inaccurate or incomplete Data under certain circumstances.
  • The right to request to erase or limit the processing of your Data under specific conditions.
  • The right to object to the processing of your Data, as allowed by applicable law.
  • The right to request to receive your collected Data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format to facilitate its transfer to another platform, where technically feasible.

To exercise these rights, please send an email to enterprise1@vishnucr9.org and follow the instructions provided. To verify your identity for security, we may request extra information before addressing your data-related request. Please contact our Data Transparency Officer at the same address for any feedback or concerns.

We aim to promptly respond to requests in compliance with legal requirements. Please note that we may refrain from processing certain data as necessary for legal obligations or for establishing, exercising, or defending legality.
